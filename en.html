About RAIDA

RAIDA is an international virtual platform for the production, exchange, and application of knowledge. It seeks to connect scholars, researchers, universities, and research institutions to identify challenges, foster constructive dialogue, and develop policy proposals.

Mission and Approach

RAIDA aims to facilitate access to reliable knowledge and transform it into insight, innovative ideas, research models, and assessable recommendations. Its approach emphasizes scientific collaboration, international dialogue, futures thinking, and real-world challenges.

Research Areas

RAIDA's activities are organized around five main research areas.

1. Women's Futures Studies

Exploring trends and future scenarios related to women, families, and social participation, while developing forward-looking strategies and policy recommendations.

2. Religious Communication and Civilizational Dialogue

Researching contemporary approaches to religious communication, intercultural engagement, interfaith dialogue, and the effective communication of religious knowledge.

3. International Seminary–University Dialogue

Supporting academic collaboration between seminaries, universities, scholars, and research institutions in Iran and around the world.

4. Health and Spirituality

Studying spiritual dimensions of health, spiritual care, human dignity, professional ethics, and the role of spirituality in patient experience and care.

5. Health Systems

Researching health policy, health-system governance, equitable access to services, quality of care, and program evaluation.

About RAIDA

RAIDA is an international virtual platform for knowledge exchange, interdisciplinary inquiry, and constructive dialogue. It seeks to connect scholars, researchers, universities, and research institutions to examine complex issues and develop informed proposals.

Mission and Approach

RAIDA aims to facilitate access to reliable knowledge and support its translation into research insights, analytical frameworks, practical ideas, and proposals that can be assessed and refined.

Its approach emphasizes scholarly cooperation, international dialogue, interdisciplinary research, futures thinking, and attention to real-world challenges. Research findings, analytical interpretations, and policy recommendations are treated as distinct forms of output.

Research Groups

RAIDA's research agenda is organized around five main areas.

1. Women's Futures Studies and Foresight

Exploring emerging trends and future scenarios concerning women, families, and social participation, with an emphasis on strategic foresight and future-oriented policy development.

2. Religious Communication and Civilizational Dialogue

Studying religious communication, intercultural engagement, interfaith dialogue, and approaches to communicating religious knowledge in contemporary societies.

3. International Dialogue Between Seminaries and Universities

Supporting scholarly exchange and collaborative research among seminaries, universities, academics, and research institutions across countries and disciplines.

4. Health and Spirituality

Exploring spiritual aspects of health, spiritual care, human dignity, professional ethics, and the role of spirituality in patient experience and care.

5. Health Systems

Examining health policy, health systems governance, equity in access to services, quality of care, health financing, and programme evaluation.

Introduction

Seminaries and universities represent important settings for the development, interpretation, and exchange of knowledge. Dialogue between these institutions can create opportunities to examine complex questions from different intellectual perspectives and connect theoretical inquiry with contemporary social needs.

This research group within RAIDA International Virtual Think Tank focuses on international academic dialogue between seminaries and universities, with attention to cooperation, mutual understanding, and interdisciplinary research.

Mission and Approach

The group's proposed mission is to explore constructive approaches to collaboration between religious and university-based institutions while respecting their different traditions, methods, and areas of expertise.

  • Identify shared questions that benefit from interdisciplinary inquiry.
  • Encourage dialogue between religious scholarship and contemporary academic research.
  • Explore models of institutional cooperation and knowledge exchange.
  • Support respectful discussion of philosophical, ethical, cultural, and social issues.
  • Examine opportunities for international research partnerships.

Research Themes

  • Comparative approaches to knowledge, learning, and research.
  • Philosophical and ethical questions in contemporary academic life.
  • Religion, science, society, and interdisciplinary dialogue.
  • Models of cooperation between seminaries and universities.
  • International academic networks and cross-cultural knowledge exchange.
  • Education, research ethics, and the social responsibility of scholarship.
  • Institutional and cultural barriers to sustained intellectual dialogue.
  • Digital platforms and new opportunities for international collaboration.

Proposed Activities

  • Academic roundtables involving scholars from different disciplines.
  • Seminars on shared intellectual and social questions.
  • Joint research proposals and comparative studies.
  • Preparation of research articles, policy briefs, and analytical reports.
  • Online dialogues and international knowledge-sharing sessions.
  • Development of conceptual frameworks for institutional cooperation.

Activities and outputs will be shaped by research priorities, participating scholars, and potential institutional partnerships.

Collaboration

University faculty members, seminary scholars, researchers, postgraduate students, and relevant academic institutions are invited to explore potential opportunities for dialogue and collaborative research.

RAIDA aims to support constructive international exchange by connecting perspectives, facilitating access to knowledge, and encouraging thoughtful engagement with complex questions.

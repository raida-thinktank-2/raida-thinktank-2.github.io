Introduction

Religious communication plays an important role in how individuals and communities understand beliefs, values, identities, and shared human concerns. In an interconnected world, constructive communication can help create opportunities for mutual understanding while allowing differences to be discussed thoughtfully and respectfully.

This research group within RAIDA International Virtual Think Tank focuses on the relationship between religious communication, cultural interaction, and civilizational dialogue.

Mission and Approach

The group's proposed mission is to explore knowledge-based and ethically responsible approaches to religious communication and dialogue among cultures and civilizations.

  • Examine theories and practices of religious communication.
  • Explore the relationship between religion, culture, identity, and society.
  • Identify opportunities for respectful dialogue across differences.
  • Study the role of media and digital technologies in religious communication.
  • Develop analytical approaches to communication challenges in diverse societies.

Research Themes

  • Religious communication theories, methods, and frameworks.
  • Intercultural and interreligious dialogue.
  • Religion, civilizational identity, and cultural interaction.
  • Digital media, online communication, and religious narratives.
  • Religious literacy and public understanding of religion.
  • Communication ethics, dialogue, and the management of disagreement.
  • Religious outreach in international and multicultural contexts.
  • Communication strategies that support cooperation and mutual understanding.

Proposed Activities

  • Academic seminars and expert roundtables.
  • Comparative research on religious communication approaches.
  • Preparation of research articles, analytical reports, and policy briefs.
  • Workshops on dialogue, communication skills, and intercultural understanding.
  • Collaborative discussions with academic and research institutions.
  • Development of conceptual frameworks and practical research tools.

Specific activities and outputs will be determined in accordance with research priorities, available expertise, and potential partnerships.

Collaboration

Researchers, communication specialists, religious studies scholars, educators, and relevant institutions are invited to explore potential opportunities for academic exchange and collaborative inquiry.

RAIDA aims to facilitate constructive international conversation through accessible knowledge, careful research, and respect for cultural and intellectual diversity.

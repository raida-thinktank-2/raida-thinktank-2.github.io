Introduction

Health systems bring together institutions, professionals, resources, policies, and services that aim to protect and improve people's health. Understanding how these elements interact is essential for examining system performance, access to care, quality, equity, and long-term sustainability.

This research group within RAIDA International Virtual Think Tank focuses on health systems through policy analysis, governance studies, interdisciplinary research, and evidence-informed inquiry.

Mission and Approach

The group's proposed mission is to support a deeper understanding of health systems and contribute to informed discussion of policies, institutions, and strategies that shape health outcomes.

  • Examine health system structures, functions, and governance.
  • Explore policy options and their potential effects on populations.
  • Study access, equity, quality, and continuity of health services.
  • Analyse the use of human, financial, and technological resources.
  • Encourage dialogue among researchers, policymakers, and health professionals.

Research Themes

  • Health policy analysis and strategic planning.
  • Health system governance, accountability, and institutional coordination.
  • Primary healthcare and integrated service delivery.
  • Equity, access, and financial protection in healthcare.
  • Quality improvement, patient safety, and person-centred services.
  • Health workforce planning, education, and professional development.
  • Health financing, resource allocation, and system efficiency.
  • Digital health, data governance, and responsible innovation.
  • Resilience, emergency preparedness, and health system sustainability.
  • Ethics, public trust, and community participation in health systems.

Proposed Activities

  • Research seminars and policy-focused expert discussions.
  • Comparative studies of health systems and governance models.
  • Preparation of policy briefs, research reports, and academic articles.
  • Workshops on systems thinking and health policy analysis.
  • Collaborative research with academic and professional institutions.
  • Development of analytical frameworks and indicators for system assessment.

Activities and outputs will be developed in line with identified research priorities, available evidence, and potential partnerships. Recommendations should take account of local contexts and the limitations of available data.

Collaboration

Researchers, health professionals, policy analysts, university faculty members, and relevant institutions are invited to explore potential opportunities for academic dialogue and collaborative inquiry.

RAIDA aims to encourage knowledge exchange and interdisciplinary discussion that can inform thoughtful analysis of health systems and their future development.

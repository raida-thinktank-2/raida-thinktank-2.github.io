Introduction

Health is a multidimensional concept that encompasses physical, psychological, social, and spiritual aspects of human life. Spirituality can be an important area of inquiry when examining how people understand meaning, cope with difficult experiences, maintain hope, and relate to others during times of illness and recovery.

This research group within RAIDA International Virtual Think Tank explores the relationship between health and spirituality through interdisciplinary research, ethical reflection, and dialogue between relevant academic and professional fields.

Mission and Approach

The group's proposed mission is to advance informed discussion and research on spiritual wellbeing and its relationship to holistic health, human dignity, and compassionate care.

  • Explore conceptual and theoretical approaches to spirituality and health.
  • Examine the role of meaning, hope, and personal values in experiences of illness.
  • Study spiritual care in relation to dignity, empathy, and professional responsibility.
  • Encourage dialogue among health professionals, researchers, and scholars of spirituality.
  • Consider cultural and ethical dimensions of spiritual wellbeing.

Research Themes

  • Spiritual wellbeing and holistic approaches to health.
  • Spiritual care and respect for human dignity in healthcare settings.
  • Meaning, hope, resilience, and coping with illness.
  • Compassion, empathy, and ethical relationships between care providers and patients.
  • Spirituality, mental wellbeing, and quality of life.
  • Cultural and religious diversity in experiences of health and care.
  • Education and professional development in spiritual care.
  • Ethical questions in the integration of spirituality and healthcare.

Proposed Activities

  • Interdisciplinary seminars and expert discussions.
  • Research reviews and comparative studies on spirituality and health.
  • Workshops on dignity-centred communication and compassionate care.
  • Preparation of research articles, analytical reports, and policy briefs.
  • Knowledge exchange among healthcare professionals and academic researchers.
  • Development of conceptual frameworks and educational resources.

Research and educational activities will be developed according to identified needs, available expertise, and potential partnerships. Research findings will be considered in light of the quality and limitations of the available evidence.

Collaboration

Researchers, healthcare professionals, educators, scholars of spirituality, and relevant institutions are invited to explore opportunities for academic dialogue and collaborative research.

RAIDA seeks to encourage responsible, evidence-informed, and culturally sensitive conversations that contribute to a deeper understanding of health, spirituality, and human dignity.

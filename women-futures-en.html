Introduction

The Women's Futures Studies and Foresight group is a research area within RAIDA International Virtual Think Tank. It focuses on understanding the changing conditions that shape women's lives, identifying emerging opportunities and challenges, and developing informed perspectives on possible and desirable futures.

The group approaches women's futures through interdisciplinary research, futures thinking, cultural analysis, and policy-oriented inquiry.

Mission and Approach

The group's proposed mission is to strengthen knowledge-based approaches to women's futures and contribute to more inclusive, resilient, and responsible social development.

  • Explore emerging trends affecting women, families, and communities.
  • Examine alternative scenarios and long-term social changes.
  • Connect research findings with policy questions and practical needs.
  • Encourage dialogue among researchers, educators, and practitioners.
  • Consider cultural, ethical, social, and technological dimensions of change.

Research Themes

  • Futures studies and strategic foresight concerning women.
  • Women's roles in education, knowledge production, and social innovation.
  • Changing family structures, intergenerational relations, and social wellbeing.
  • Women, emerging technologies, and transformations in work and learning.
  • Cultural identity, social participation, and women's agency.
  • Future-oriented policy design and the assessment of alternative scenarios.
  • Opportunities and challenges facing women in local and international contexts.

Proposed Activities

  • Research seminars and expert discussions.
  • Collaborative studies and interdisciplinary research projects.
  • Futures workshops, scenario development, and trend analysis.
  • Preparation of policy briefs, research reports, and analytical articles.
  • Knowledge exchange with academic and research institutions.
  • Development of research questions and practical frameworks for future studies.

Activities and outputs will be developed according to research priorities, available partnerships, and the group's evolving programme.

Collaboration

Researchers, university faculty members, students, policy analysts, and relevant institutions are welcome to explore opportunities for academic dialogue and potential collaboration in women's futures studies and strategic foresight.

RAIDA seeks to facilitate dialogue across disciplines and contexts through accessible knowledge and constructive international exchange.
